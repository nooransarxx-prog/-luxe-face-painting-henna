About Luxe

Two crafts, one studio, London-wide

Luxe Face Painting & Henna is a London-based studio bringing together two distinct art forms — vivid, playful face painting and fine, ornamental henna. Every booking is treated as a bespoke commission, whether it's a children's birthday, a festival stall, or a bride's most important day.

We work with professional, skin-safe materials and a calm, reliable approach that event organisers, venues and families trust to show up prepared and deliver on time, every time.

  • Kids Parties

    Birthday parties, school fetes and family fun days, big or small.

  • Festivals & Vendor Events

    Full-day festival coverage and vendor market bookings across London.

  • Bridal & Cultural Henna

    Bridal mehndi, Eid celebrations and culturally rooted design work.
